Assumes fees stay at today's USD rates, the pool reward moves only with difficulty, and your farm doesn't change except through reinvesting. Reinvested TH get the chosen miner's efficiency at a fixed price per TH, discounts stay at today's percentage as the farm grows, and reinvested TH aren't counted in resale value. The token discount uses GoMining's published table (1% per 18 days of fees covered, up to 20% at 360 days). Miner Wars prizes, promos and token price swings are not modeled.

Fee, discount and reinvest rules from GoMining Docs: Maintenance fees and discounts and Rewards. Live data from mempool.space. Estimates for planning only, not financial advice. For a single miner, use Miner Calc.
